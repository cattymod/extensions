How to use Modals

1. Click Download to open CattyMod Studio.

2. Click the Add Extension button in the bottom-left corner.

3. Click the CattyMod category.

4. Select Custom Extension.

5. Open the Files tab.

6. Enable Run without sandbox.

7. Upload the extension file.
